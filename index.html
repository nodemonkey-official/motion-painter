Core workflow

From gesture to animation.

Motion Painter brings path creation, targeting, motion design, rotation, and output control into one visual workflow inside Blender.

Paint paths

Draw motion directly in the viewport.

Draw a freehand guide or click connected segments with Motion Polygon. Work on the view plane, at the 3D Cursor, or across visible surfaces. Use Chosen Surface to project onto one mesh with an adjustable offset; pixel-based spacing keeps your strokes predictable at different scene scales. See the paper-plane demo.

Motion modes

Rail and Forcefield motion

Rail mode follows your guide, with keys based on editable control points or distance spacing. Forcefield mode shapes movement around the stroke with pull, push, orbit, wave, and jitter. Start with a direct route, then give it your own character.

RailForcefieldOrbitWaveJitter
Stack + blend

Build complex motion in layers.

Stack multiple guides and decide how they interact. Takeover lets a higher path assume control over a smooth frame range; Add combines its flow with motion from lower paths.

TakeoverAddBlend framesPath order
Targets

Direct one object or a whole group.

Pick nearby objects automatically in screen or 3D space, or use your own selection. Hierarchy Roots targets the highest included parent when its children are also inside the brush. Add or exclude objects as you refine the group, with improved motion handling for animated parents.

Auto SelectHierarchy RootsParented objectsAdd + exclude
Rotation

Control how objects turn

Face the final direction of travel, look toward the path, or add spin. Bank into turns and blend from the original pose, or use Keep Offset to preserve starting facing. Nose Axis, Roll Reference, and a labeled axis gizmo help you set up directional rotation.

Follow directionLook at pathSpinBanking
Curves + editing

Edit a guide as a Blender curve

Turn a guide into a linked Blender curve, or create a guide from an existing curve. Refine its shape with Blender's curve tools and let the animation update. Version 1.0 improves linked editing, follows evaluated NURBS centerlines, and includes the closing segment of cyclic polyline paths.

Clean output

Editable guides. Cleaner keyframes.

Simplify location and rotation keys within your chosen tolerances. Inspect movement with continuous speed colors and trace guide contributions with colored key outlines in the Timeline and Dope Sheet. Finalize the motion to keep ordinary Blender keys for further editing.

Since the launch beta, Motion Painter has gained Chosen Surface drawing and guide-colored key overlays. The full release brings these new tools together with clearer paths, more timing control, and a stronger animation foundation.

New / Chosen Surface

Let the surface shape the journey.

Choose a mesh and paint a route across its surface. The chosen mesh stays out of your animation targets.

Surface Offset. Adjust Offset to lift your painted path away from the chosen mesh, following the surface normal.

See it guide a car over uneven terrain. Inspired by a suggestion from our Discord community—join us and share your ideas.

New / Colored key overlays

Connect the keys to the motion.

Color Guide Keys matches Timeline and Dope Sheet key outlines to their guides. Stacked contributions show multiple colors.

Paths In Front. Paths In Front keeps your guides visible through scene geometry. Switch it off to let paths respect depth instead.

Also shown: existing Simplify Keyframes, Speed Colors, Visible Padding, and the Motion Guide layer system.

Timing

Shape the pace, too.

Preserve the rhythm of your stroke with Draw Speed. Brake before bends with Turn Slowdown and control recovery with Acceleration. Forcefield Speed Up and Slow Down adjust the start and finish without changing the trajectory.

Existing animation

Refine with more confidence.

Improved ownership tracking restores displaced source keys when generated motion is removed. Shared actions are copied before writing, so animating one target does not change another object using the same action.

Everyday editing

Keep your changes in step.

Undo and redo keep guide settings and generated animation consistent. Failed rebuilds restore the previous result, while linked-curve edits and parented motion previews update more reliably.

Roadmap

Motion Painter is just getting started.

Can I edit the generated keyframes?

Yes. Active guides manage their generated keys and can rebuild them when you change the setup. When you want to continue by hand, use Keep Keys, Remove Path. Guides connected through shared targets are finalized together, preserving their combined animation.

Can I animate large groups?

Use manual selection or Auto Select, with Hierarchy Roots to avoid targeting both included parents and children. New strokes have a 256-target limit and additional workload checks. If a stroke exceeds the limits, reduce the targets, shorten the path, or increase its speed.
