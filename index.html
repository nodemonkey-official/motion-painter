From gesture to animation.

Motion Painter brings path creation, targeting, motion design, rotation, and output control into one visual workflow inside Blender.

01 / Paint paths

Draw motion directly in the viewport.

Drag to create a fluid freehand guide or click connected segments with Motion Polygon. Project strokes on the view plane, at the 3D Cursor, or directly across visible surfaces.

03 / Motion modes

Follow the rail or feel the field.

Rail mode places motion directly along your guide. Forcefield mode treats the stroke as a flowing influence, with pull, push, orbit, wave, jitter, slowdown, bend handling, and turn controls.

04 / Stack + blend

Build complex motion in layers.

Stack multiple guides and decide how they interact. Takeover lets a higher path assume control over a smooth frame range; Add combines its flow with motion from lower paths.

05 / Targets

Direct one object or a whole group.

Automatically pick nearby objects from the start of a stroke in screen or 3D space. Add and exclude selected targets at any time, then select every target again with one click.

Movement that knows where to look.

Generate orientation from the final motion using Follow Direction, Look At Path, or Spin. Blend from the original pose, preserve an offset, bank into turns, and choose the exact local axes.

Stay visual. Stay editable.

Create a linked Blender 3D Curve from a guide, or build motion from a selected curve. Select paths in the viewport, erase control points, rename guides, change colors, reorder the stack, and toggle output previews.
